About

I am a Professor of Community Health and Public Health Nursing at the Faculty of Nursing, Al-Baha University, with more than eighteen years of experience in academia, healthcare research, innovation and health systems strengthening across the Middle East and Africa.

My background covers nursing, public health and policy, strategic planning, nursing education, quality improvement, emergency preparedness and global health research. I currently head the Medical-Surgical Nursing Department and supervise the Master’s in Nursing program, and I lead multidisciplinary, AI-enabled healthcare innovation projects.
